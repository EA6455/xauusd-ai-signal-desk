How it works

Real-time spot gold (exchange websocket feed, spot-anchored) with a composite AI score in [−1, +1] per timeframe — blending a trained logistic-regression model (45%) with a classic rule ensemble (55%): EMA 9/21 trend, SMA-20, MACD, RSI and Bollinger bands.

  • Multi-market, TradingView-style — 12 markets (gold, silver, 7 majors + 3 crosses) × 15 timeframes, drawing tools (trend lines, rays, horizontals, rectangles, Fibonacci retracement, text — auto-saved per market), candle color themes, and a ⧉ dual-screen mode to watch two markets side by side. Gold keeps the realtime tick stream; other pairs poll live quotes every 10s.
  • Strategy concepts — beyond the core SNR retest: 🧹 liquidity sweeps (wick through a zone + reclaim, EMA200-aligned only — stops taken), 📐 HTF ladder (15m setup inside a 4H zone upgrades the card), ⏸ event black-out (no new signal alerts 30 min before → 15 min after high-impact USD events).
  • AI Analyst Desk — eight independent models read the same chart (neural ML, momentum, market structure, SNR zones, mean reversion, volatility & range, live order flow, macro) and vote; the consensus meter aggregates them, confidence-weighted. External LLM analysts (GPT · Claude · Gemini · Groq) take extra seats automatically when their API keys are configured — plus one 🌅 AI daily brief on Telegram.
  • SNR Entry (15m) — graded setups: A+ (8/8, tracked + phone), B+ (7/8, phone with warning), C+ (6/8, phone with warning) — plus ⏳ setup-forming heads-ups when price taps a fresh zone. Malaysian-style Supply & Demand: market structure (HH·HL / LH·LL), break of structure, the origin zone that caused it, first retest with engulfing/pin confirmation, 1H trend agreement, SL beyond the zone, TP1 at 1:0.75 RR — and never during dead hours.
  • Signal Tracker — every signal card (A+/B+/C+ retest and ⚡ momentum) is tracked live to its final TP or SL: half banked at TP1 (+0.38R), stop to breakeven, runner to TP2 (+1.13R). You get alerts at every step, and the outcome history builds up on the card.

Fundamentals — economic calendar (CPI, FOMC, NFP...), dollar index & US 10Y yields, and gold-relevant headlines filtered from WatcherGuru + Google News. High-impact events send a pre-alert 30 minutes ahead; breaking gold news is forwarded (rate-limited).

Prices are spot-aligned to match XAU/USD at your broker. Backtest stats shown on the cards are measured on the last 60 days of 15m data.
