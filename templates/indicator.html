PROOF — Self-Proving Zones · measured, not promised

The first indicator that proves itself. Every zone label carries its own measured track record, computed live on your chart's own history: FRESH · 1st bounce 68% (n=24) · TESTED (1) · next 41% · WEAK (3) · 12% · break map. On load it replays the whole chart, finds every zone it would have drawn, and scores every touch honestly: a bounce counts only if price travelled the target ATR in the rejection direction before the zone broke — closed bars only, no repaint. Timeouts are excluded, small samples are flagged low n — never fake numbers.

every label = its own track record base rate vs raw levels · edge proven no signals · no alerts · closed bars only road map: next zones + NEXT STOP

Scoreboard (top-right, toggleable): 1st / 2nd / 3rd+ touch bounce rates, the same test on raw pivot levels (base rate), and the edge in points — proof the zones beat random levels on your timeframe. Options: bounce target (ATR), evaluation window, low-sample threshold, scoreboard position, demand/supply filters, colors, zone caps. Pairs with FLOW15mn: run PROOF beside them and see which zones deserve your trust.

Road map — which box is price going to? The scoreboard's last rows show the next zone ↑ and ↓ (level, state, measured %, ATR distance), the NEXT STOP row names the likeliest destination — the nearest untested FRESH magnet — and dotted path lines point to both zones on the chart. A measured map of where price can travel, not a prediction: trade toward the next stop, expect the reaction AT the box.
How to trade it — the PROOF method
  1. Read the scoreboard first. "1st touch" % is your headline number. "Edge vs raw" +10 pts or better = these zones beat random levels on this chart — trade them. Near zero = stand aside or switch timeframe. Everything "low n" = not enough history yet — don't trade the numbers.
  2. Trade only FRESH zones with strong stats. Wait for price to return to the box. Enter at the zone edge (limit order) or on a confirmation candle close at the edge. Stop beyond the far side of the box. Half off at ~0.75R, stop to breakeven, runner to 1.5R.
  3. Downgrade as the numbers downgrade. TESTED (n) shows the NEXT-bounce % — if it reads ~40%, that's a coin flip: smaller size, confirmation candle, TP1 only. WEAK = never buy the bounce — use it as a breakout map (trade the push through it, stop on the far side).
  4. Vet your signals with it. A+ or REV signal from the desk (Telegram) printing from a FRESH zone with good stats = the full plan. Signal at a TESTED zone = reversal style, smaller. Nothing at a WEAK zone — skip.

The % is the measured bounce probability on THIS chart — pair it with the desk's exit plan for your R. Numbers grow as history accumulates; re-check the scoreboard weekly. Not financial advice.

Best timeframes — 15m main (the whole stack is measured on 15m, and FLOW15mn signals on 15m so vetting works on one chart) · 1H cleaner-but-slower alternative · 4H structure/bias (zones live for weeks, stats mature slowly) · avoid 1–5m — noise and dead-session wicks wreck zones. The honest answer is in the scoreboard: compare the edge row across timeframes — the TF with the highest edge is the best one on your feed. More history loaded = more samples (scroll back, re-check).

CLOCK — Time-of-Day Proof · when zones deserve trust

PROOF answers WHICH zones deserve trust. CLOCK answers WHEN. It replays your chart's own history, finds every zone the PROOF engine would draw, and scores every touch by the hour of day (UTC) it happened in. Session table: Asia 00–04 / 04–08 · London 08–12 · NY 12–16 / 16–20 · Late 20–24 — measured bounce % + n each. Plus best hour & worst hour (named, with sample size) and zone overall vs the raw pivot-level base rate.

PROOF for time · stacks on one chart best & worst hour — measured heat tint: green ≥60% · red ≤40%

Heat tint: every bar is shaded with its hour's measured % — walk-forward, the heat you would have seen then. How to use: compare hours before trading a zone bounce — green-hour touches only, avoid red hours. Stack with PROOF: FRESH zone + strong hour = the A-setup; fresh zone in a weak hour = wait. 15m main, same as the stack. Same honesty rules: bounce = target ATR before break, closed bars only, timeouts excluded, low n flagged. No signals, no alerts.
